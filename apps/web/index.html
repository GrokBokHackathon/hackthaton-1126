How It Works

1 🏴‍☠️

Grab the Flag

Rush to the enemy base and touch their flag to pick it up. WASD on desktop, joystick on mobile. Don't get tagged!

2 🏃💨

Run It Home

Sprint back to your base with the flag. Watch out—enemies can tag you in their territory and send you back!

3 🏆

First to 3

Score three captures before the other team. New procedural map each round. Pure party chaos. No chill allowed.
