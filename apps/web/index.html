FLAG RAID

Humans vs Grok Bots in a silly realtime Capture the Flag battle. Steal the flag, dodge enemies, bring home the chaos. 🎉

🎮 Play Now 📦 View Repo

How It Works

🏃

Grab the Flag

Rush to the enemy base and touch their flag to pick it up. WASD on desktop, joystick on mobile.

🏠

Run Home

Sprint back to your base with the flag. Watch out—enemies can tag you in their territory!

🏆

First to 3 Wins

Score three captures before the other team. New procedural map each round. Pure party chaos.
