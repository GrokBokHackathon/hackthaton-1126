## Wall bitmask: N=1, E=2, S=4, W=8
Index = the sum of the bits for 4-neighbours that are walls (map edge counts as wall). Frame `wall_%02d` at x=index*32, y=32.
| idx | conn | idx | conn | idx | conn | idx | conn |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | pillar | 4 | S | 8 | W | 12 | S+W |
| 1 | N | 5 | N+S | 9 | N+W | 13 | N+S+W |
| 2 | E | 6 | E+S | 10 | E+W | 14 | E+S+W |
| 3 | N+E | 7 | N+E+S | 11 | N+E+W | 15 | all |
```js
const m=(w(x,y-1)?1:0)|(w(x+1,y)?2:0)|(w(x,y+1)?4:0)|(w(x-1,y)?8:0);
```

## Map size, camera and fog of war (added 2026-11-05)

**Map:** 64x40 tiles by default (2048x1280 px at source), configurable in the generator. The bases sit in opposite diagonal corners, about 60 tiles apart in a straight line. The player never sees the whole map at once, and the camera follows their unit.

**One view for every device:** everyone gets the same field of view in tiles, not in pixels, so a phone and a browser are equally fair.
- The vision radius is 7 tiles from the unit (a circle). Bushes block sight lines, and walls block sight lines.
- The camera zoom fits 16 tiles onto the shorter side of the screen: `zoom = shortSidePx / (16 * 32)`. On a landscape desktop that's height 16 tiles and width as much as fits. On a portrait phone that's width 16 tiles and height as much as fits.
- If `zoom >= 2`, round it down to a whole number and use nearest-neighbour. Below 2, use fractional zoom with smoothing on. The art is flat emoji with soft edges, so that still looks clean.
- The UI (score, flag indicator, buttons) is drawn in screen space at its own scale and doesn't zoom with the map.

**Fog of war, 3 states per tile:**
1. Unexplored: solid `#1A1020` (shadow) at 100%.
2. Explored but not visible now: terrain shown with `#1A1020` at 60% on top. Enemies, flags carried by enemies, and power-ups are NOT drawn (the last known flag position may show as a ghost icon).
3. Visible: no overlay.
- The fog edge is soft: one 32x32 radial-falloff brush stamped per visible tile into a low-res fog mask (1 px per tile), scaled up with smoothing. No fog tileset is needed.
- The server decides visibility and sends each client only the enemies it can see, so fog can't be removed from the browser.
- Optional minimap: 1 px per tile at 2x, showing only explored tiles, teammates, and your own flag.

**New art:** none required. Optional P2 items: `fog_edge_brush_32.png` (a white radial gradient, alpha only), `ghost_flag_16.png` (the flag outline at 50%).

## Procedural map rules (reference: mapgen_reference.py, seeded, 64x40)
1. **Symmetry:** generate one half, then mirror every tile by 180° rotation around the centre: (x,y) → (W-1-x, H-1-y). Bases sit at (4,4) and (59,35).
2. **Routes:** at least 3 between the bases. (a) A long safe lane along the outer edge. (b) A short risky lane through the centre, with mud and the hedge maze next to it. (c) A water lane that crosses the stream via a bridge. Add 2–3 side objectives (centre, hackathon camp, pond edge) with spur paths.
3. **Winding:** each route = random-walk waypoints (every ~1/4 of the way, jittered ±4–6 tiles), joined by A* over a smoothed noise cost field. The A* state includes (direction, run length), so **no straight segment is longer than 5 tiles**, and the run carries across waypoints.
4. **Clusters, not singles:** scatter in clusters of 3–7. Trees (2x2) ×9 clusters, rocks, tall grass, flowers, crates, a camp of desk/rack/keg/pizza/sign/cable, a pond plus a 1-wide stream, a 9x7 DFS hedge maze, and short or L-shaped wall segments.
5. **Clear zones:** keep a 5x5 clear area around each base, no trees within 5 tiles of a base, nothing blocking on a path tile (paths are locked), and no wall within 3 tiles of a base.
6. **Density:** blocking tiles (wall, water, rock, tree, props, crate) target 25–35%. Add wall segments until ≥27%, and undo any segment that breaks connectivity.
7. **Connectivity:** flood fill from the red base. The bot base must be reachable, along with ≥97% of the walkable tiles; otherwise reject the segment or the seed.
8. **Placement rules:** water mask counts water+bridge; path mask counts path+bridge+mud; walls use their own mask. Bridges go only where a path crosses water (bridge_h if the path runs E-W, else bridge_v), ideally on 1-wide crossings (the stream). Trees never sit on paths and stay ≥5 tiles from bases. Mud goes only on the risky route.

Results: seed 1 → 27.2% blocking, 99.5% reachable. Seed 2 → 27.2% blocking, 97.8% reachable. Both have a longest straight run of 5.
